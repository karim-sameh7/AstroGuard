The Problem ASTROCARE SolvesNASA HRP Classified

Space missions expose the human body to conditions fundamentally different from life on Earth. NASA classifies these under the Human Research Program (HRP):

Microgravity
Fluid shifts, cardiovascular deconditioning, SANS risk in >70% of crew
Space Radiation
GCR & SPE DNA damage, cancer risk, acute radiation syndrome
Isolation & Confinement
Psychological stress, circadian disruption, cognitive degradation
Sleep Disruption
Circadian rhythm misalignment, performance impairment
Limited Medical Support
4-24 min radio latency to Earth, up to 14-day blackouts during solar conjunction
ASTROCARE's Differentiator: We don't just display health values — we correlate them with mission conditions and track their changes over time, enabling truly autonomous clinical decision support.

NASA HUMAN RESEARCH PROGRAM · PROBLEM STATEMENT & SYSTEM MISSION

What Critical Problem Does AstroGuard Solve?

1. THE CORE PROBLEM: DEEP SPACE MEDICAL AUTONOMY CRISIS
On deep-space exploration missions (Lunar Gateway, Artemis Base Camp, and Mars Transit), astronauts operate outside Earth magnetosphere and hundreds of millions of kilometers from terrestrial hospitals:
  • Communication Latency & Blackouts: Radio signals take 4 to 24 minutes one-way to reach Mars (up to 48 minutes round-trip), plus solar conjunction blackouts lasting up to 14 days. Real-time terrestrial telemedicine is clinically impossible during acute cardiac arrhythmias, SANS intraocular spikes, or acute trauma.
  • Multi-System Physiological Hostility: Microgravity redistributes ~2 liters of venous fluid cephalad, crushing the optic nerve (SANS in >70% of crew), deconditioning the left ventricle, and resorbing bone mineral density at 1.5% per month.
  • Absence of Specialist Physicians: A crew of 4 to 6 cannot carry an oncologist, ophthalmologist, cardiologist, and radiologist.
2. THE ASTROGUARD SOLUTION
AstroGuard transforms raw biometric sensor telemetry into actionable clinical flight interventions without relying on Earth:
  • Multi-Layer Anatomical Digital Twin: Real-time mapping of 9 vital streams to specific organ systems with predictive risk modeling.
  • Evidence-Grounded CDSS: Automated correlation with official NASA Human Research Roadmap countermeasures (LBNP decompression, ARED axial loading, circadian lighting, and storm shelter protocols).
  • Autonomous Store-and-Forward Telemetry: Clinical SBAR handoff packets generated automatically for delayed ground transmission.
  • Dynamic Crew Registration: Multi-astronaut calibration allowing custom baseline profiling for any crew member.
